Member control

Delete your account and data

Members can remove individual submissions or request deletion of their full Spearfishing Victoria website account.

Delete an individual submission

  1. Sign in to the Spearfishing Victoria website.
  2. Open My account, then My submissions.
  3. Choose the submission and select Delete.

Request full account deletion

  1. Open the Spearfishing Victoria Facebook community and contact an administrator.
  2. State that you want your website account deleted and provide the email address used to sign in.
  3. For your security, an administrator may ask you to confirm control of the account.

After verification, we will delete or anonymise your website profile, sign-in record and associated personal information. We will also remove unpublished submissions and uploaded images. Approved public contributions may be removed or anonymised as appropriate.

Facebook permissions

You can also remove Spearfishing Victoria from Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites. Removing the Facebook connection does not by itself delete information already stored by this website, so use the account-deletion process above if you want the website data removed as well.

Processing time

We aim to acknowledge deletion requests promptly and complete verified requests within 30 days. Some limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, dispute resolution or legal obligations.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

What to include

Your community display name.

The email address used for the website account.

A clear request to delete the account and associated data.
