Challenge rules

Fair, verifiable and responsible

  1. Victoria only. The catch must be taken in Victorian waters during the challenge window.
  2. Follow current law. Size, bag, protected-area and equipment rules always come first.
  3. Your best catch counts. Only the largest approved entry per member appears in the standings.
  4. Clear evidence required. The full fish and measurement must be readable in the submitted photos.
  5. Moderation is final. Entries may be declined when timing, location, measurement or capture method cannot be verified.
