Privacy policy

How the Spearfishing Victoria website collects, uses and protects member information.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we receive basic account information from your chosen sign-in provider. This may include your name, email address, profile picture and provider account identifier. We also store information you choose to add to your community profile.

When you participate, we store your votes, catch and recipe submissions, uploaded photos, moderation history and related community activity. We do not ask members to publish exact fishing locations.

How we use information

  • To authenticate members and keep submissions linked to the correct account.
  • To operate moderation, community voting, leaderboards, recipes and member profiles.
  • To prevent misuse, investigate technical problems and keep the community safe.
  • To display approved contributions where the member has chosen to participate publicly.

Sign-in providers

Google and Facebook sign-in are provided by those companies. Spearfishing Victoria receives only the account information authorised during sign-in and does not receive your provider password. Their own privacy policies also apply to their services.

Sharing and public content

We do not sell personal information. Approved catches, recipes, display names, profile photos and optional Instagram handles may be visible publicly when submitted or enabled by the member. Moderators can access pending and rejected material as required to operate the website.

Storage and security

Account records, submissions and images are stored using Supabase and the website is hosted using Netlify. Reasonable technical controls are used to restrict private uploads and administration features, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can update profile visibility and delete eligible submissions from your member account. You can also request deletion of your account and associated personal information using the instructions on our data deletion page.

Contact and changes

Privacy or deletion questions can be sent to the Spearfishing Victoria administrators through the existing Facebook community. This policy may be updated as website features change.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

In plain language

We use your information to run member accounts and community features.

We do not sell your information or publish precise catch locations.

You control optional public profile details and can request account deletion.
